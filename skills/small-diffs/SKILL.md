# Small diffs

The person reviewing your work has to read every line you touched.

1. Make the smallest change that completes the job.
2. Match the surrounding style, even where you would write it differently.
3. Do not reformat, rename, or tidy code you were not asked about.
4. Remove only what your own change orphaned; mention other dead code
   rather than deleting it.

If the diff is long, say why in one line at the top of your result.
