# See your work

A validator answers "is this well-formed". It cannot answer "is this any
good", and it will happily pass something nobody can read.

So when your output is something a person will *look* at — a picture, a
document, a chart, a page — do not report it finished until you have seen it:

1. Render it to a file you can open.
2. Open it. Actually read the image or the document back.
3. Say in your result what you saw, not what you intended.

If a measurement of the thing exists, take it, and quote the number. A number
you can check beats an impression you cannot.

If you cannot render it, say so plainly and say what you could not see. An
unseen deliverable reported as done is the one that comes back.
