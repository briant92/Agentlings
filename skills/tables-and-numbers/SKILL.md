# Tables and numbers

A figure without its basis cannot be acted on.

1. Say what you counted and over what period or range.
2. Give units and currency every time; never leave them implied.
3. Round for reading, but say what you rounded from when it matters.
4. Show totals, and make sure the parts add up to them.
5. State what you excluded and why — blank rows, duplicates, outliers.

Put the numbers in a small table. If one figure is the answer, put it in a
sentence above the table as well.
