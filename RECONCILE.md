## 2. What professionals reconcile against

The **book side** — the cash/bank account in the general ledger, the company's
own register of the same account. Invoices, bills and payroll are what the book
entries were built from, not the counterpart. Where no ledger exists, the
counterpart is the documents it would have been built from: in Chile the SII's
*Registro de Compras y Ventas* (every DTE issued or received; a semicolon CSV per
period with folio, fecha, RUT, razón social, neto, IVA, total), plus the
recurring items no register carries (payroll, interest, fees).

The output a CPA signs: two columns landing on one number — the bank balance ±
items the books have and the bank has not (deposits in transit, outstanding
cheques/transfers), the book balance ± items the bank has and the books have not
(fees, interest, returned items, errors; each needing an entry) — with the
matched list, the unmatched list and sign-off. Monthly; stale items aged.

The matching ladder every tool uses: amount first, a date window next
(QuickBooks: 90 days back, 20 forward), then reference text (folio, RUT,
document number, payee), with confidence tiers and one-to-many groups (a
processor payout net of fees; a fee split across lines).

## 3. The rules this line works under

- **One method, many counterparts.** The ladder, the two-sided statement, the
  roll-forward and the equality invariant are fixed; what the reference field
  is, what the book side may adjust, and the output vocabulary are read off
  the files.
- **Match in code, judge in a session.** The matching runs as a script inside
  the sandbox and prints the residue; the session spends its turns on what the
  residue means. Three hundred statement lines need never enter the context.
- **The invariant is the gate.** Adjusted balances equal, or nothing is
  reconciled; the server computes it, never trusting a file's claim.
- **A `clear` writes no state** (D-216), and any new await in the promote path
  re-reads the job's status after it (D-162/D-163).
- **Reading money, never moving it** (D-219).

**What the three runs teach**

1. **Decision 4's premise was false.** The analyst is not turn-starved:
   `turnCapFor` lets the quote's budget override the role's standing
   `maxTurns` (D-067), and a $2 ceiling at Haiku prices granted the 40-turn
   ceiling. Eleven, nine and twelve turns sufficed. No widening, no new trade.
2. **The method transfers; the invariant does not.** Every run matched in a
   kept script, categorised the residue sensibly and adapted to the
   counterpart it was given — but only run 2 produced an equation, and none
   produced the two-sided statement. The deliverable must be **asked for**:
   that is B3's contract, and it may not need a skill at all.
3. **The recipe drifts toward the last counterpart.** One sentence, one key,
   three counterparts: each close-out *replaced* the approach (D-073's rule
   applied to recipes). After run 3 it says bank fees are *out-of-scope* —
   true against an invoice register, wrong against a ledger, where they are
   book-side adjustments needing entries. The model survived the drift
   because it reads the files; a compiled tool would not, and the recipe
   sits **one success away from compiling** the last shape into a $0 tool.
   This is B1, measured.
4. **The matcher knows the job.** The desk planned `analyst` at confidence
   0.43 on the verb alone; no vocabulary change, no replay owed.
5. **Real counterparts are richer than textbooks.** The one-to-many fee match
   and the open-invoice reading both happened on the first try; the fixtures
   should keep those shapes when they become tests.

**What it changes in the plan**

- **B1 first, and before any fourth run of that sentence.** Attachment shape
  joins recipe identity; a compile keyed on the sentence alone would freeze
  the wrong shape.
- **B3 becomes contract-first.** Ask for `RECONCILIATION.json` in the brief —
  both balances, matched pairs, unmatched per side with category, proposed
  entries — check it at parse, compute the invariant on the server, refuse
  Approve when it does not balance. Mount a skill only if a measured run
  still misses the statement with the contract in hand (D-190's bar: a skill
  costs ~14% a turn).
- **B2 drops in priority.** The runs read their files whole and never needed
  the desk's help; the arrest for *a statement and nothing to reconcile it
  against* stays worth having, later.
- **B4 unchanged.**
- The three trial jobs and the two probe jobs sit in Training Ground's
  review; **Clear** them (D-216) — a discard would bank a refusal into Dot's
  memory that no one meant.
